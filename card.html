PANDA

Panda's are peaceful, black and white bears and strictly on Bamboo diet.

  • Scientific Name: Ailuropoda melanoleuca
  • Avg Length: 1.2 to 1.9 meters
  • Avg Lifespan: 15 to 20 years in the wild
  • Habitat: Bamboo Forests in mountains of southwestern China.

They are excellent climbers and swimmers.
